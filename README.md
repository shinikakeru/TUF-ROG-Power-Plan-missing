**If your power plan or Power mode synchronization ( option in Armoury crate) is missing on either TUF or ROG laptop you can watch this repository to possibly find a solution to bring them back.**

# How to fix it?
## There are 2 ways. Depends on your conditions. I will separate them later.
<img alt="Guide with arrows to all ways to fix issue" src="Pictures/Guide.png">

# Steps
## 1 Step
**install Armoury Crate uninstall tool**
<br>**Link:** https://www.asus.com/supportonly/armoury%20crate/helpdesk_download/

**Tap Show all and scroll down until you find it**
<img alt="screen picture 1" src="Pictures/ScreenPic1.png">
<img alt="screen picture 2" src="Pictures/ScreenPic2.png">
**Unzip rar folder to desktop and run Armoury Crate Uninstall Tool.exe with admin rights**
<img alt = "screen picture 3" src="Pictures/ScreenPic3.png">

**Wait for it to uninstall and restart your PC**

## 2 Step
**Win+R and type rstrui.exe**
<br>
**Click OK**
<br>
<img alt = "screen picture 4" src="Pictures/ScreenPic4.png">

**Click next and you will see dates of restore points, try to remember when problem appeared**

**If you don't have any restore points here -> skip to step 2.1**
